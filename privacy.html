Vishnupriya's Collections

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who we are

Vishnupriya's Collections runs a private management app for its own shop. Only the shop's owner and staff use it. Contact: vpscollections2025@gmail.com.

Google account access

The app asks for permission to Google Drive using the drive.file scope only. With it, the app can see and change only the files it creates itself: the shop's backup files in a "VPC Backups" folder and copies of product photos in a "VPC Photos" folder. It cannot see, read or change any other file in the Drive.

What the app stores

The backups contain the shop's own business records — bills, stock, suppliers and customer contact details the shop keeps for billing. They are stored on the shop's server and in the shop's own Google Drive. They are not sold, shared or given to anyone else, and are not used for advertising.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to save and restore the shop's backups.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's Third-party connections page. The backup files stay in the Drive until deleted there.
